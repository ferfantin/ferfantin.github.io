Button-making Witch in Guildford, UK

Hail, mortal, nice to meet you!

My name is Fernanda, and I am a UI Designer working at Larian Studios.

I have a bachelor's degree in fashion design, but being so passionate about games made me pursue a career in creating interactive experiences instead!

I develop mockups alongside Game Designers and the UI team, create high-fidelity assets, and fully implement the UI. I also create game logos, non-UI 2D art and custom fonts on some occasions, which you can check in this portfolio!

This is an interactive space I developed to share my work and, by extension, to be a portfolio piece by itself. So please, make yourself at home! (But take your shoes off first!)
